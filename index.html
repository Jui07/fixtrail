DDR4 SODIMM RAM Slot & Contacts

The Dell Inspiron 15 5567 uses standard DDR4 2400MHz SODIMM memory. In hostel environments with humidity or after laptop drops in backpacks, fine dust and microscopic copper oxidation prevent clean address pin contact.

Realistic Visual Check: Symptom: 3 Amber, 2 White
8GB DDR4
Fingernail press on both side clips → Module pops up at 30°
Required Senior Action:
  • Disconnect battery connector cable before touching RAM.
  • Use two thumbs to spread the silver clips outward until the stick springs up.
  • Clean the gold fingers firmly with a clean white pencil eraser or 99% IPA.
  • Seat back firmly into DIMM Slot A at a 30° angle until you hear a sharp mechanical snap.

Hostel Evidence: Reseating memory and cleaning copper oxidation resolved 14 of 19 identical Dell 5567 "black screen with spin-down" tickets in Pune colleges.
